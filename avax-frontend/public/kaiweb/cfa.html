Getting started

  1. Step 1

    Join

    Sign in with Google or email and join your CFA.

  2. Step 2

    Set up

    Your admin adds your nursery groups and the species you grow.

    Record

    Get verified

    Your CFA verifier approves; the record is timestamped.
