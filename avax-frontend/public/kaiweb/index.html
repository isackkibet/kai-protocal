How a record becomes trusted

  1. Step 1

    Record

    A CFA member records nursery or planting work.

  2. Step 2

    Check

    A CFA verifier checks it and approves it.

  3. Step 3

    Timestamp

    A fingerprint of the record is saved on Avalanche.

  4. Step 4

    Publish

    Anyone can read it and check it was never changed.

What a mural tells you

Which CFA

The Community Forest Association behind the work.

Who planted

The members who raised and planted the trees.

When

The dates of planting and checking.

The proof

A link to each record and its Avalanche timestamp.
