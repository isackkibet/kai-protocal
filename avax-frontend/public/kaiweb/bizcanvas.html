Business model · 1 min read

Murals pay for the work. Hubs make it trusted.

We sell conservation murals and portraits, and offer Information Hubs as a service to CFAs and partners.

See the model

How KAI earns

Murals and portraits

Sold from about KES 15,000, each with its verified story.

Information Hubs

A hub for a CFA or conservation group, set up and supported.

Commissions

Portraits and murals made to order for homes, offices and events.

Who we serve

Buyers of art

People and companies who want art with real impact.

CFAs

Forest groups that want their work recorded and recognised.

Partners

NGOs, funders and counties that need trusted conservation data.

Why it works

Simple to run

No exchange, no tokens to trade: art and information services.

Trust built in

Every story is checked and timestamped on Avalanche.

Value to communities

The work of the planters is visible and part of every sale.
