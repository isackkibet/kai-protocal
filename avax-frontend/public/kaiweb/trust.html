KAI Trust

Protect your wealth. Pass it on with confidence.

Set the rules once. Your wealth follows them, for your family and beyond.

Open the app

How it works

  1. Step 1

    Create

    Start from a template: family, education, retirement, business or charity.

  2. Step 2

    Set the rules

    Who gets what, and when.

  3. Step 3

    Fund it

    Add savings, investments or tokenized assets.

  4. Step 4

    It is managed

    Reports and AI insights; a licensed trustee where the law requires.

  5. Step 5

    It pays out

    When the conditions happen, like a graduation or a date.

Who is who

Trustor

Creates and funds the trust, and sets the rules.

Trustee

Runs it by the rules. Licensed where required.

Beneficiaries

Family, children, charities or groups who receive it.

Enforcer (optional)

Checks the trustee follows the rules.

Built on five promises

Ownership

Your assets follow your instructions.

Transparency

Every action is recorded.

Security

Strong cryptography protects records.

Compliance

Works with licensed trust professionals.

Automation

Less paperwork, fewer mistakes.
