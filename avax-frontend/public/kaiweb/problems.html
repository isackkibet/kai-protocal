The problem · 1 min read

People work hard. The system doesn't help them build wealth.

Payments move money, then stop. Nothing grows and nothing is protected.

See the gapsNext: Our solution

Four big gaps

Payments don't build wealth

Money arrives and sits. Savings, insurance and pensions live in separate apps.

Made for one person, not groups

Chamas, SACCOs and forest groups still run on paper books.

Valuable assets stay locked

Sales records and forest work don't count when you need a loan.

Crypto is too hard

Seed phrases and gas fees keep most people out.

Who pays the price

Small businesses

Real sales every day, but no credit.

Forest associations

Chamas and SACCOs

Big savings, eaten by inflation and fraud risk.

Young Africans
