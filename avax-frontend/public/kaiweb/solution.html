How it works · 1 min read

From a nursery record to a proof anyone can check.

Provenance is the history of something: who made it, where, and when. We give conservation work a history that can't be changed.

See the journeyNext: Murals

The journey of one record

  1. Step 1

    Recorded

    A nursery group enters seedlings, planting or a survival check, by form or by talking to Kanuvari AI.

  2. Step 2

    Checked

    A CFA verifier reviews it, with photos as evidence.

  3. Step 3

    Fingerprinted

    The record gets a unique fingerprint (SHA-256).

  4. Step 4

    Timestamped

    Fingerprints are saved on the Avalanche blockchain.

  5. Step 5

    Published

    The record appears in the hub with a proof page.
