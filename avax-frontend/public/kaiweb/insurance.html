KAI Insurance · 1 min read

Let your savings pay your insurance.

Put tokens to work. What they earn pays your premium to a licensed insurer.

See how it worksNext: Pension

KAI is not an insurance company. Your policy always stays with a licensed insurer.

How it works

  1. Step 1

    Choose your cover

    Health, motor, business, property, life or farm, from a licensed partner.

  2. Step 2

    Put in tokens

    They stay yours.

  3. Step 3

    KAI works out the amount

    Riskier cover needs more savings working for it.

  4. Step 4

    Earnings pay the premium

    Automatically, with a record of every payment.

  5. Step 5

    Grow over time

    As savings grow, more of the premium is covered.

Your savings cover more each year

10%of the premium in year 1
35%in year 2
60%in year 3
100%from year 5

Example only. Depends on how the assets perform; not guaranteed.

What you get

Licensed insurers

KAI pays them; they cover you.

Your money stays yours

Take it out any time. If it drops too low, you are warned before a payment is missed.

One pot, many policies

One savings pot can pay several covers.

AI tips

Help with saving and affordability, never with claims.
